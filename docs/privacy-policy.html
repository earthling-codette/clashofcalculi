Clash of Calculi Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Clash of Calculi collects, uses, and protects information when you use the game. Clash of Calculi is a math battle game with optional account, sync, advertising, and purchase-related features.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the game, we may collect:

  • Account information, such as your email address, if you create an account.
  • Gameplay and progress information, such as scores, solo results, rewards, profile progress, and sync records.
  • App activity and diagnostics, such as interactions, crash logs, performance data, and device or app information.
  • Advertising-related identifiers and ad interaction data when rewarded ads are shown.
  • Purchase-related records handled through Google Play Billing if you make a purchase.

How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Provide and operate the game.
  • Save progress, rewards, and account-related data.
  • Support optional online sync and account features.
  • Show rewarded ads and measure ad performance.
  • Process purchases through Google Play Billing.
  • Understand crashes, performance issues, and gameplay problems.
  • Prevent abuse, fraud, and unauthorized activity.

Third-Party Services

Clash of Calculi may use third-party services such as Google Play services, Google Play Billing, Google AdMob or advertising services, and Supabase for account or backend functionality. These services may process data according to their own privacy policies and terms.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share information with service providers that help operate the game, including hosting, authentication, advertising, billing, analytics, crash reporting, fraud prevention, and platform services. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect users, the game, or our rights.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect information. Data transmitted between the app and backend or platform services is intended to be encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. No system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Children

Clash of Calculi is intended for users age 13 and older. The game is not directed to children under 13.

Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your account and associated data by following the instructions on our Account and Data Deletion page.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the effective date above.
