Clash of Calculi Account and Data Deletion

Effective date: May 4, 2026

You can request deletion of your Clash of Calculi account and associated data by contacting the developer.

How to Request Deletion

  1. Email bumblepopstudios@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line: Delete Clash of Calculi Account.
  3. Include the email address used for your Clash of Calculi account.

Data We Delete

When we process your request, we delete account data associated with your account where applicable, including:

  • Account profile data.
  • Saved gameplay progress.
  • Solo results and reward progress.
  • Backend sync records linked to your account.

Data We May Keep

Some records may be retained if required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, billing records, tax or accounting obligations, dispute resolution, or enforcement of our terms. Required retained records may be kept for up to 90 days unless a longer period is legally required.

Processing Time

We will respond to deletion requests within a reasonable period after verifying the account information needed to process the request.

Privacy Policy

For more information about how Clash of Calculi handles data, read the Privacy Policy.
